How to Find Your Folder ID

  1. Open Google Drive in your browser
  2. Navigate to the folder you want to use
  3. Look at the URL in your browser's address bar: drive.google.com/drive/folders/XXXXX
  4. Copy the XXXXX part - that's your folder ID
For shared folders:
  • Make sure you have "Editor" access to the folder
  • The folder owner can share it from Drive's share menu

How to Find Your Spreadsheet ID

  1. Open Google Sheets in your browser
  2. Open the spreadsheet you want to use
  3. Look at the URL in your browser's address bar: docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/XXXXX/edit
  4. Copy the XXXXX part - that's your spreadsheet ID
Requirements:
  • You must have "Editor" access to the spreadsheet
  • Extension will auto-create "TikTok Download Log" sheet tab if missing
